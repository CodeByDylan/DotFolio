# Cookie statement

This site uses two cookies. Both are created only when you change a setting, and neither one is used
to track you.

## What is stored

**folio.locale** is created when you pick a language. It holds a language code such as `en` or `nl`,
so the site still shows that language when you come back. It expires after one year.

**folio.theme-mode** is created when you pick light, dark, or system appearance. It holds that
choice, so the site looks the same when you come back. It expires after one year.

Neither cookie holds your name, your email address, or any identifier that points to you. Neither is
sent anywhere except back to this site.

## What is not here

There is no analytics on this site. No visitor counter, no heatmap, no session recording, no
advertising, and no third party tracking cookies. Nothing measures what you read or how long you
stay.

Nothing else is kept in your browser either. The site does not use local storage or session storage.

## One request that leaves this site

The fonts come from Google Fonts. Your browser asks `fonts.googleapis.com` and `fonts.gstatic.com`
for the font files, and those requests carry your IP address and basic details about your browser.
Google sets no cookies for them, but the requests themselves are visible to Google. This is the only
third party your browser contacts while you read this site.

## Removing the cookies

You can delete both cookies at any time in your browser settings, and the site keeps working without
them. It will fall back to the language your browser asks for and to your system appearance setting.
